Can You Finance Another Car After a Repossession in Ontario?

Yes. An auto repossession—or a voluntary surrender where you handed the keys back to the lender—is recorded as an R9/I9 rating on your Equifax and TransUnion credit bureaus for up to 6 years. Because it directly involves an auto loan, prime banks and standard dealership finance managers almost always issue an automatic decline.

However, AutoBanker maintains direct underwriting relationships with specialized Canadian second-chance and post-repossession lenders across Toronto, the GTA, and Ontario. These lenders understand that job loss, divorce, medical emergencies, or an overpriced predatory loan from another dealer can happen to anyone. Instead of judging your past repo, they underwrite your current gross income ($1,800+/month minimum) and stability today.

🔑 The 3 Keys to Post-Repossession Approval in Ontario: 1. Time Since Repo: If your repossession occurred more than 12 months ago—or was included in a consumer proposal or bankruptcy—you qualify for multiple lender tiers immediately.
2. Recent Recent Repo (< 12 Months): If the repo happened within the last year, a modest down payment ($500–$1,500) or clean 90-day bank statements with zero NSF fees unlocks specialized approval programs.
3. Right-Sized Vehicle Payment: Keeping your new payment under 15% of your gross monthly income guarantees lender confidence.

Voluntary Surrender vs. Involuntary Repossession: Does It Matter?

Many Ontario drivers are surprised to learn that Equifax and TransUnion code both a voluntary surrender and an involuntary tow-truck repossession as an I9 (Installment Account Charged Off / Repossessed). However, when a human underwriter at one of AutoBanker's partner lenders reviews your file, a voluntary surrender—or a deficiency balance that has been settled or placed inside a consumer proposal—is viewed much more favorably.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct underwriting answers from AutoBanker's Ontario finance desk.

How soon after a car repossession can I get another car loan in Ontario?

If your repossession was included in a consumer proposal or bankruptcy, or if at least 6 to 12 months have passed with stable employment and clean bank statements, AutoBanker can get you approved right away. Even more recent repossessions can be approved with a modest down payment or co-applicant.

What if I still owe a deficiency balance on my old repossessed car?

If the previous auto lender sold the car at auction and left a remaining deficiency balance, our underwriting team will route your application to a different specialty lender (never the same bank that held the old loan) that allows prior auto deficiencies.

Do I need a down payment for a car loan after repossession?

If the repossession is over 12 months old or discharged inside a proposal/bankruptcy, $0 down options are often available. For a very recent repossession (under 6–12 months), putting $500 to $1,500 down or trading in a vehicle significantly increases approval odds.

Will a new car loan erase the repossession from my credit report?

While the old repossession remains on your credit report for 6 years from the date of first delinquency, adding a new auto loan paid on time every month proves to future lenders that you have rehabilitated your credit, rapidly lifting your score.
