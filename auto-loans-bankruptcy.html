Can I get a car loan after a consumer proposal?

Yes. At AutoBanker, we understand that financial setbacks happen. Whether you are currently in a consumer proposal or have recently been discharged from bankruptcy, our Toronto-based auto financing team has established programs designed specifically to get you approved.

Absolutely. An auto loan is one of the fastest and most effective ways to rebuild your credit profile in Canada. We work with lenders who report your positive payments directly to the major credit bureaus, helping you establish a strong financial future while driving a vehicle you love.
