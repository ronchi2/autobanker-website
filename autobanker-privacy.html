Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

AutoBanker ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other applicable Canadian privacy laws. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (autobanker.ca) or use our auto financing lead generation services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with auto financing solutions, we must collect personal and financial information. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and physical address.
  • Financial & Employment Information: Monthly income, employment status, employer name, job tenure, and housing status.
  • Vehicle Preferences: Desired vehicle type, budget, and trade-in details.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction data via cookies and tracking pixels (e.g., Google Analytics).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Evaluate your auto loan eligibility.
  • Match your profile with our network of trusted automotive dealer partners and third-party lenders.
  • Contact you via phone, SMS, or email regarding your application and financing options.
  • Verify your identity and protect against fraud.
  • Improve our website functionality and customer experience.

3. Consent to Contact & Credit Inquiries

By submitting your application and completing the verification process, you explicitly consent to AutoBanker and its affiliated dealer partners/lenders contacting you via SMS, phone call, or email. You also authorize our lending partners to obtain credit reports from credit reporting agencies (such as Equifax or TransUnion) to assess your creditworthiness.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to marketing agencies. We only share your data with:

  • Lending & Dealer Partners: Specifically chosen partners who can facilitate your vehicle purchase and loan approval.
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist with our website operations, data routing, and secure communications.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: If required by law, subpoena, or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including 256-bit SSL encryption, to protect your personal information during transmission and storage. However, no electronic transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Under Canadian law, you have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and request corrections if it is inaccurate. You may also withdraw your consent to future communications by replying "STOP" to our SMS messages or clicking the "Unsubscribe" link in our emails.
