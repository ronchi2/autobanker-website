Credit Rebuilding

Your credit score shouldn't dictate your future. We specialize in structuring auto loans that report positively to major Canadian credit bureaus.

By securing a vehicle through AutoBanker and making consistent, on-time payments, you actively rebuild your credit profile, paving the way for lower interest rates in the future.

VIP Refinancing

Are you stuck in a high-interest auto loan? If your credit has improved since you initially purchased your vehicle, we can help.

Our VIP Refinancing service analyzes your current loan and matches you with lenders willing to buy out your old contract and issue a new one at a significantly lower interest rate, saving you thousands.

Vehicle Sourcing

Looking for something specific? Through our expansive network of elite dealership partners across the country, we can source exactly what you are looking for.

Once you are pre-approved, simply tell us the make, model, and year of the vehicle you desire, and our acquisition team will find it for you.

Cash Back Loans

Consolidate your high-interest debt (like credit cards) directly into your auto loan. Our Cash Back auto loans allow you to borrow more than the value of the vehicle, giving you cash in hand.

This allows you to pay off expensive debts and manage a single, easy-to-track monthly payment at a much lower blended interest rate.
