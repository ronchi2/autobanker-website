Can I get approved for a car loan while on ODSP?

Absolutely. If you receive ODSP (Ontario Disability Support Program), CPP (Canada Pension Plan), or other guaranteed fixed incomes, we have lenders who specifically cater to your situation. We understand that reliable transportation is essential.

Will my monthly payments be affordable?

Yes. Our finance experts will carefully structure your loan to ensure that your monthly or bi-weekly payments fit perfectly within your fixed income budget, without causing financial strain.
