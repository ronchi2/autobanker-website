What Is a Zero Down Car Loan?

A zero down car loan means you get a vehicle without paying any money upfront. You finance 100% of the car's price. This includes the vehicle cost, taxes, and sometimes even fees. You just make monthly payments instead.

Many people think you need a big down payment to buy a car. That is simply not true. Thousands of Canadians get approved for $0 down car loans every year — including people with low credit scores.

💡 Quick Fact: A down payment is money you pay before you start the loan. A zero down loan skips that step entirely. Your savings stay in your pocket.

How Does Zero Down Financing Work?

When you apply for a car loan, the lender looks at a few things. They want to know: Can you afford the monthly payments? Here is what they check:

  • Your monthly income — how much money you bring home each month.
  • Your debt-to-income ratio — how much of your income is already going to other bills.
  • Your credit score — a number that shows how reliable you have been with past loans.
  • Your employment history — how long you have been working at your current job.

If these things look good, the lender will cover the full cost of the car. You make monthly payments over a set number of months. At the end, the car is yours.

Zero Down vs. Down Payment: What's the Difference?

Both options let you get a car. But they work differently. Here is a simple comparison:

Factor Zero Down Loan Loan With Down Payment
Money needed upfront ✔ $0 $500 – $5,000+
Monthly payment amount Slightly higher ✔ Slightly lower
Total interest paid A little more ✔ A little less
Savings stay in your account ✔ Yes ✗ No
Good for emergency fund ✔ Yes ✗ Not always
Speed to approval ✔ Fast Fast

Paying a small amount down can reduce your monthly payment a little. But if you need your savings for other things — like rent, food, or an emergency — a zero down loan makes a lot of sense.

Can I Qualify for Zero Down With Bad Credit?

Yes. Many of our clients have low credit scores or no credit history at all. We work with lenders who focus on your income and your ability to pay — not just your credit score.

Here are the general requirements for zero down approval:

  • You have a steady income of at least $1,800/month.
  • You live in Ontario or another Canadian province.
  • You have a valid driver's licence.
  • You can show proof of address (a utility bill or lease agreement works).

Even if you have gone through a bankruptcy or a consumer proposal, we may still be able to get you a zero down loan. We look at your full picture — not just a number.

What About the Interest Rate?

Zero down loans sometimes come with a slightly higher interest rate. This is because the lender is taking on more risk. But here is the good news: if you make all your payments on time, your credit score will go up. After 12 to 24 months, you may be able to refinance at a lower rate.

AutoBanker shops your application to dozens of lenders at once. This means we find the lowest rate you qualify for — saving you money every month.

Tips to Get the Best Zero Down Deal

  • Choose a reliable used car — a lower vehicle price means lower monthly payments.
  • Keep the loan term reasonable — a 60-month term is often the sweet spot between low payments and less interest.
  • Never miss a payment — every on-time payment builds your credit score.
  • Avoid add-ons you don't need — extended warranties and insurance add-ons raise your loan amount. Only get what you really need.
  • Use a broker, not a single dealer — a broker like AutoBanker shops your deal to many lenders so you get the best rate.
