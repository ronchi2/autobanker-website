Comprehensive Auto Financing in Toronto & The GTA

At AutoBanker, we know everyone's money journey is different. Getting a car loan in Toronto and across Canada should not be stressful, no matter your credit score. Our goal is to connect you with the right lender to get you the best loan. We make buying a car clear and easy.

Types of Auto Loans We Offer

Bad Credit Car Loans

Life happens. Missing payments or sudden events can lower your credit score. We are experts in bad credit car loans. We work with lenders who look past your score to get you driving. This helps you fix your credit with monthly payments you can afford.

New Immigrant Financing

Are you new to Canada? Having no credit history here should not stop you from getting a car. We have special programs for permanent residents, students, and work permit holders. We help you get your first Canadian car loan with great rates.

Bankruptcy & Consumer Proposals

Even if you have gone through bankruptcy or a consumer proposal, you can still get a car loan. In fact, getting a car loan is one of the fastest ways to fix your credit after money troubles.

Zero Down Payment Loans

Do you have no money saved up? That is okay! Our Canadian lenders often approve car loans with $0 down. This lets you get a car right away while keeping your cash in the bank.

Why Choose a Specialized Auto Broker?

Going to a normal car lot with bad credit can lead to many credit checks. This hurts your score and can get you rejected. As an auto broker, we check your file once. We then send it to the exact lender who will approve you. This protects your credit and gives you the best chance to get a car loan.
